Community Colleges Are Economic Development

A stronger local economy depends on more than attracting new businesses. It also depends on helping people gain skills, supporting entrepreneurs, and connecting employers with the talent they need.

Read the original article ↗
Why it matters in Brunswick County

Build opportunity close to home

Community colleges are practical partners in economic development. They help residents prepare for in-demand careers, give local businesses access to training and advice, and create pathways to better jobs without requiring every student to leave the region.

For a growing community, these services are part of the infrastructure that helps local people and local employers succeed.

01 / WORKFORCE

Prepare people for good jobs

Career-focused education and workforce training can help residents develop the skills employers are looking for—from technical and professional skills to industry-specific credentials.

02 / SMALL BUSINESS

Help businesses grow

Small-business support can help entrepreneurs explore an idea, strengthen operations, build management skills, and find practical guidance as their businesses develop.

03 / ACCESS

Make education more attainable

Affordable local learning options and scholarship opportunities can reduce barriers for students and help more residents pursue education and career goals.

A local resource

Brunswick Community College connects education with economic opportunity

Brunswick Community College serves the region through education, workforce and business services. Its Small Business Center is one resource for people starting or operating a small business, while career and technical programs help learners build skills for the workplace.

Scholarship programs, including the Brunswick Guarantee, can also be an important part of the conversation about access to higher education. Students should check current eligibility rules, covered costs, and application deadlines directly with the college.
